Fraud intelligence platform

From a detected anomaly to a signed finding, with the evidence for every step.

Call records are ingested and checked against detection rules. When a rule fires, an analyst opens a case, asks each source for evidence and tests the competing explanations. The platform shows what the records prove, what they only suggest, and what is still missing, so nobody acts on suspicion alone.

Every claim carries its level

    Only a level 5 finding, verified by evidence from outside the analysis, can be signed. A supervisor who is a different user must countersign before anything is referred. Every action is written to a hash-chained audit trail.
