About

A telecom fraud intelligence platform built from published research.

A telecom fraud intelligence platform built from published research. This demo runs on synthetic data and is not connected to any operator. Not yet reviewed by telecom practitioners.

What is live and what is prepared

Live in this demo

Ingesting call records, running detection rules over every record, the alert queue, opening a case, the case workflow, the level gates, roles, the hash-chained audit trail and the referral packet. These work on any feed in the same record format.

Prepared in this demo

What each outside source returns: device information, radio measurements, registration data and the field survey. No operator is connected, so each source answers through a demo connector that serves synthetic data. A live connector replaces it without changing anything else.

All records, people, organisations and places in the demo are synthetic. Organisations are named the way an intelligence product minimises them, for example Operator 1. No real company or authority is a client, a source or a recipient.

Five levels of claim

Every statement belongs to exactly one level. Each level is shown three ways: the numeral, the name and a mark. Colour never carries it.

    Six basis labels

    Levels describe claims. Basis labels describe where a rule, field, mechanism or role comes from.

    Evidence sources in the model

    Call records E1
    Charging records from the serving call-control node, outgoing and incoming. Field model from ETSI TS 132 260 Table 6.1.3.3.1; every value synthetic.
    International gateway records E2
    Charging records at the network border, per route. Field model from ETSI TS 132 260 Table 6.1.3.10.1. How the traffic arrives is an assumption of the model.
    Device information E3
    Device-model fingerprints from signalling, never from call records. Method after Oh et al., NDSS 2023; collecting it is an assumption.
    Subscriber registration E4
    Administrative records held by another team, requested by the analyst. Assumption.
    Radio measurement E5
    Distance from the antenna in timing-advance steps of about 78 m. Assumption: it appears in no charging record.
    Field survey and test call E6
    Evidence from outside the analysis. In the demo it is prepared synthetic data.
    Number-range reference data E7
    Tariff and range reference. The need for it is industry practice.
